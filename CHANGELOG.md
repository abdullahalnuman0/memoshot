# Changelog

Release notes for **Memoshot** on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.memoshot).

## 1.3.1 · November 2026
- **Fixed:** the app could ask for your fingerprint when opening, even with app lock turned off.
- **Fixed:** *Add tag* is no longer hidden behind the navigation bar.
- Ads keep their place, so nothing moves while they load. Full-screen ads only appear after you finish something, like a cleanup.

## 1.3.0 · October 2026
- Ads now come from Google's newer ads library and load in the background, so they never slow the app down.
- Full-screen ads only appear after you finish something, like a cleanup.
- Small fixes.

## 1.2.0 · October 2026
- **Tags:** group screenshots your way and filter by tag.
- **Search widget** for your home screen.
- **Share images to Memoshot** from other apps.
- Read **Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese and Korean** text (turn them on in Settings).
- Optional **code alert:** copy a new one-time code from the notification; it never shows on the lock screen.
- Optional **weekly cleanup reminder**.
- No photo access? **Pick screenshots by hand.**

## 1.1.0 · October 2026
- Every feature is now free for everyone: extra folders, custom cleanup rules and text export included.

## 1.0.0 · October 2026
- First release: on-device text recognition, automatic categories (Codes, Receipts, Tickets, Chats, Products, Other), full-text search with highlights, one-tap actions and safe cleanup of used codes, past tickets and duplicates.
